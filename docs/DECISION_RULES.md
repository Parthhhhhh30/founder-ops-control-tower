# Deterministic demo policies

These are demo policy choices, not accounting standards, legal advice or eligibility assessments.
Invoice: 3 days before due prepare reminder; due today confirm; overdue 1–6 ACT; 7–13 REVIEW; 14+ ESCALATE to finance/founder. Dispute, discrepancy or accountant dependency imposes at least REVIEW. Paid invoices close collection and create reconciliation/check work. Dates and payment truth are never AI-owned.
Hiring: missing interviewer feedback creates operator follow-up. Progression and hiring decisions belong to the named hiring owner; never rank candidates.
Starter: READY iff all mandatory checklist items complete. Missing contract or RTW review escalates to people owner; other gaps require review. RTW completion records a human review, not an eligibility conclusion.
Contracts: any non-standard clause ESCALATE to legal. Missing signature, date or template metadata REVIEW. Comparisons report differences only; never safe-to-sign language.
RTW: expired evidence or missing mandatory evidence ESCALATE; follow-up within 60 days REVIEW. Administrative tracking only.
Company: normal item + approved supplier + approved budget ACT. New vendor, unusual purchase or over budget REVIEW. Safety, access or major disruption ESCALATE.
Founder queue: unresolved decisions explicitly owned by Founder only. Other accountable owners retain legal, finance and hiring decisions unless assigned to founder.
Forward look: deterministic dated threshold crossings and incomplete commitments within inclusive 7/14 calendar-day horizons. Includes invoice 14-day escalation, missing interview feedback at decision date, starter readiness at start, unresolved renewal, RTW follow-up, event blockers and low stock reorder date. Closed sources excluded.
